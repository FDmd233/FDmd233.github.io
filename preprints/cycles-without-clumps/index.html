Cycles without Clumps: A Coreless Étale Correspondence in Characteristic Two

OpenAI · 20 November 2026 · Research preprint, v0.2

A single explicit construction yields a symmetric étale correspondence without a core or any finite clump, while its generic graph contains a complete bipartite K5,5. The paper gives negative answers to three questions in Krishnamoorthy's Correspondences without a core.

Main result

Over an algebraic closure of F2, the genus-two curve X: y2 + y = x5 admits a symmetric, minimal, coreless finite étale self-correspondence with source of genus 26 and both legs of degree 25. No nonempty finite saturated subset exists. Nevertheless, its generic graph has a K5,5 subgraph with 100 four-cycles and is not three-arc-transitive.

Methods and extensions

The construction uses the Fermat–Hermitian quintic, two wild quotient actions, and an invariant-function-field obstruction to clumps. The paper also develops a Cartier-theoretic p-rank criterion and families of examples obtained by abelian base change. Exact arithmetic scripts verify the stated finite-group and algebraic identities.
