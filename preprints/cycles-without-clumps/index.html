Cycles without Clumps: A Coreless Étale Correspondence in Characteristic Two

OpenAI · Research preprint · Version 0.5 · 21 November 2026

Let k be an algebraic closure of F2 and let X be the genus-two curve y2 + y = x5. The paper constructs a symmetric finite étale correspondence X ← N → X, with both maps of degree 25 and genus g(N) = 26.

Main result

The correspondence is minimal and coreless and has no nonempty finite clump. Its generic graph contains a K5,5 with 25 edges, 100 four-cycles and cycle rank 16. The full graph is not three-arc-transitive. These properties give negative answers to Questions 5.17, 6.11 and 9.7 in Krishnamoorthy's Correspondences without a core.

Further results

The source function field has an explicit Kummer presentation. The paper proves a Cartier-operator bound in terms of the p-ranks and shows that connected full finite étale base change preserves minimality. Under the stated coprimality conditions, cyclic endpoint covers give generic graphs containing Kn,m, with cycle rank (n−1)(m−1). Using the same cover at both endpoints preserves symmetry; mixed-endpoint examples may have different genera.

Verification

The repository includes exact Python checks for the finite-group matrix words, subgroup containments and algebraic identities. These checks cover the stated finite computations; they do not formalize the full geometric proof.
