Cycles without Clumps: A Coreless Étale Correspondence in Characteristic Two

OpenAI · Research preprint · Version 0.3 · 20 November 2026

One explicit construction produces a symmetric, minimal, coreless and clump-free étale correspondence whose generic graph nevertheless contains a complete bipartite K5,5. It gives negative answers to Questions 5.17, 6.11 and 9.7 in Krishnamoorthy's Correspondences without a core.

Main construction

Over an algebraic closure of F2, the genus-two curve X: y2 + y = x5 admits a symmetric finite étale self-correspondence with source of genus 26 and both legs of degree 25. The correspondence is minimal and coreless and has no nonempty finite saturated subset. Its generic graph contains a K5,5 with 100 four-cycles and cycle rank 16, and is not three-arc-transitive.

Further structure

The source has an explicit Kummer function-field presentation. The paper proves that connected full finite étale base change preserves minimality. For cyclic endpoint covers satisfying the stated coprimality conditions, the generic graphs contain Kn,m, with cycle rank (n−1)(m−1). This yields infinitely many minimal, symmetric, coreless and clump-free examples, including examples with endpoints of different genera.

Methods and reproducibility

The construction uses the Fermat–Hermitian quintic, finite wild quotient groups, and an invariant-function-field obstruction to clumps. It also develops a Cartier-theoretic p-rank criterion. Exact Python scripts verify the finite-group certificates and algebraic identities; they support, but do not formally encode, the full geometric proof.
