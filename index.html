Selected projects

  • Erdős Problem 906: sparse Fock series

    2026 · complex analysis · Lean 4

    An explicit sparse Fock construction with quantitative localization of zeros of high derivatives and a formalized p = 3/2 theorem package.

  • A Realization of G2 for Litt Problem No. 13

    2026 · algebraic geometry

    A construction of a theta surface whose convolution Tannaka group is G2 in its seven-dimensional representation.

  • A Realization of F4 for Litt Problem No. 13

    2026 · algebraic geometry

    A realization of the simple group of type F4 as a convolution Tannaka group arising from an integral surface in an abelian fourfold.

  • Prill-Exceptional Étale Covers from Raynaud Bundles

    2026 · algebraic geometry

    Finite étale covers for which every fibre divisor moves in a pencil, constructed using Raynaud bundles and finite monodromy.

  • Multiplicative Area Potentials for Edge-Point Assignments in Convex Polygons

    2026 · discrete and computational geometry

    A multiplicative potential proving termination of Bui's edge-point swap process, with capacitated and subdivision extensions.

  • The Strengthened Szeged-Wiener Conjecture

    2026 · graph theory

    A proposed proof accompanied by an exact finite certificate; the repository states its verification status and scope explicitly.
